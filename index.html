How to Play

Take turns placing X and O. Get three in a row (across, down or diagonally) to win.

  • Play with a Friend: invite a friend online with a link or room code, or take turns on the same device.
  • Play vs Computer: you are X and go first. Choose a difficulty:
    • Easy picks random squares.
    • Medium mixes smart moves with random ones.
    • Impossible uses the Minimax algorithm to look ahead at every possible outcome. It never loses, so the best you can do is a draw.
